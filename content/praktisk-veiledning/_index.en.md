## What would we like input on?

The market dialogue concerns digital solutions for coherent services for children and young people (SAMT-BU). Topics and questions are set out in the announcement on [Doffin (2026-109852)](https://doffin.no/notices/2026-109852) and in the background information on [digdir.no](https://www.digdir.no/sammenhengende-tjenester/markedsdialog-om-felles-loft-sammenhengende-tjenester-barn-og-unge/8146).

A more detailed description of the questions we would like to have explored will also be posted under [Submissions and dialogues](/en/ekstern-markedsdialog/innspill-og-dialoger/) in due course.

## How to contribute

### Directly on the website (preferred)

The preferred way is to contribute directly on this website – as a suggested change to existing pages, a proposed new page, or a comment. Input and dialogue that take place here are regarded as a fully-fledged alternative to traditional records management (journalføring). We are working on official approval of the solution for this purpose.

Detailed guidance on the different contribution methods, including step-by-step instructions, can be found in [How to contribute](/en/hvordan-bidra/).

You may contribute in Norwegian or English.

### By email

It is also possible to send input by email to the address given under [Contact us](/en/om/#contact-us). Input received by email will be recorded in the records management system (journalført) and later reproduced and referred to by Digdir under [Submissions and dialogues](/en/ekstern-markedsdialog/innspill-og-dialoger/).
